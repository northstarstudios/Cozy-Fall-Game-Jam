# Rooting Around

In this Game, you're an great old tree, and the villagers that live close by just so happen to keep needing your help to cross over gaps, so you help them using your roots.

## Controls:

- WASD to move
- Space to check your answere to the the puzzle.

## How to play:
To complete each level you need to align your thicker root segments in a way that the villagers can go across.
